Photo Gallery

    We would like to share some photos of the historical area and the previous years "Passport in Time" work weeks held in August of each year. Much of the funding and all of the labor comes from members and supporters of the Alpine Tunnel Historic Association. The photos are courtesy of Ray Rossman, Jerry Clark, Steve Lorenz, and Gary Schmauch. Gary's photos are from September of 1992. They show the area before the restoration efforts were started.
