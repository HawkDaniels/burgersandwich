Hanging Out In Silverton/Ouray
    Sunday August 20th, 2000
    Today's report will be a disappointment for the off-road audience. We found after making the trek to Ouray that we were all just a little burned out. We did manage a trip to Silverton to play tourist, and take the standard photos of the Silverton Train. We hope to have recovered by Monday!
