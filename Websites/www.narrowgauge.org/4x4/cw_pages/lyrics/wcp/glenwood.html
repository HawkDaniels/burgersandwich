Glenwood Canyon

    By Bill Fries & Chip Davis

    This is a song about rivers...
    And a canyon, where I like to go...
    While I still have the time...

    When rivers ran blue up on the mountain
    And rivers ran green out on the plain
    When the river ran red in Glenwood Canyon
    It was only a matter of time...

    Then rivers ran gold upon the mountain
    And rivers ran wild out on the plain
    When the river ran free through Glenwood Canyon
    It was only a matter of time...

    And the miners came and they took the gold
    And the people staked a claim
    And builders came and they made a dam
    And the people came to play
    And the dozers came and they built the road
    And the people found a way...

    Through Glenwood Canyon...

    Now rivers run grey upon the mountain
    And rivers run brown out on the plain
    And the river runs red through Glenwood Canyon
    But it's only a matter of time...
