Historical Paper Items from the S.R.R. and S.N.R.R.
Various Collections

The following paper documents are from the Silverton Railroad and Silverton Northern Railroad. I find these interesting in that they provide us a glimpse into the day to day operations of the railroads. They are a kind of snapshot in time if you will. I have had an interesting time while trying to decipher the hand writing on a few of these. If you find I have missed something feel free to send me an email with your interpretation.

The waybills are from both mine and Bill Borrello's collections. Bill was also kind enough to share the wine list and stationary from his collection.
