-=- Salish Vest -=-

Most of the designs in this vest were taken from traditional Salish sweaters. They can be worked in bas relief or in a contrasting color. Designs included are: Salmon, Eagle, Corn and Coyote.

-=- Pueblo Manta Vest -=-

This design was taken from woven mantas or shawls which were worn wrapped as a dress or kilt. The design is clouds and rain with green bars representing MotherEarth. Other symbols included are Corn and a Kachina mask.
