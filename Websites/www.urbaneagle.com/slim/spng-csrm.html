Carson & Colorado - Southern Pacific Narrow Gauge
Plans available from the CSRM
Last update: 98-07-07

The California State Railroad Museum in Sacramento, CA has a library that houses massive amounts of material dealing with all aspects of railroads and railroading, particularly those in California and the West. They offer a reproduction service for their materials, and the following is a listing of the plans and drawings that are in their collection that pertain to the Carson & Colorado / Southern Pacific narrow gauge. The librarian and staff are very helpful in getting copies of these items for your personal use and research. Please write or call the museum for current reproduction prices.

California State Railroad Museum Library
111 "I" Street
Sacramento, CA 95814
(916) 323-8073
