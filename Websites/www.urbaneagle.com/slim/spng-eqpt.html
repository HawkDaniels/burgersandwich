Carson & Colorado - Southern Pacific Narrow Gauge
Surviving Equipment
Last update: 98-07-09

There is still a lot of equipment scattered around. David Coscia had a start for this list on his web page of surviving SP equipment. I added to it from the museum web pages I could find (see my links page), and Cliff Mestel provided information from Ferrell's book on the SPng. Orin Palmer verified equipment at Laws 6-98. Some additional information is from Dale Darney.

I'd appreciate any other location info anyone has that isn't on here, or verification of car locations.
