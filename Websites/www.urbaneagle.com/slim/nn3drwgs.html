SLIM RAILS - Nn3 Drawings - 97-08-28



This will be the repository for drawings I generate for narrow gauge locomotives, rolling stock, and related structures. Other drawings related to narrow gauge and drawings for trackwork will eventually find their way here.

I have a GIF of the narrow gauge section of the Pacific Desert Lines layout by the SDSoNS in San Diego's Balboa Park. The letters at the ends of the areas match together to show the entire line. It is about 89 feet from the USG mine to the Town of Tourmaline. All track in visible areas is code 40 hand laid. In the hidden areas it is Z scale flex track. To see how the narrow gauge part of the layout fits in with the rest of the layout, see the GIF of the full layout.

Here is a template for #6 narrow gauge turnouts, to be constructed from code 40 rail. The instructions for building the turnouts is on 'da Trains!'

Other drawings of narrow gauge equipment will appear here at whatever intervals it takes to get them drawn up.
