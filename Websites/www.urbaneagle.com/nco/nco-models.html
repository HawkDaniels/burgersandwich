Commercial Models Reasonably Suitable for N-C-O Modeling
by Tom Armstrong

I have not kept up with all the models available in all scales to represent the N-C-O Ry. Generally, look for models representing the Florence & Cripple Creek, and some from the Southern Pacific narrow gauge and Nevada County Narrow Gauge. Some of these models have "part numbers" or other designations other than just the number and name of the prototype, but I don't know those numbers. If you know of models or model/part numbers which belong on this list, please send me an E-mail with the data. Tom Armstrong -- nco.railfan@gmail.com
