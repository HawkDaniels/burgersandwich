Plans and Drawings for Modeling the Nevada-California-Oregon Railway
by Tom Armstrong

The following plans can be used to model the N-C-O Ry. Some of them were drawn as N-C-O equipment, and some for N-C-O equipment either before or after it was on the N-C-O. The rolling stock changed little as it passed from railroad to railroad, except for the lettering. The locos changed the most, with new pilots, headlights, boilers, cabs, tenders, etc. As with all modeling, it helps to have a photo of the genuine article in front of you as you work. Yes, I know that good original photos are hard to come by . . ..

Some of these plans are probably still available commercially, and others you may have to beg from your friends. I have listed as many as I can, so if you find one or the other, you will still have something to go by. Also, some of these plans are "better" than others. Since original data is hard to come by, these will have to do until something better comes along. If you know of other N-C-O equipment plans, please let me know: Tom Armstrong - nco.railfan@gmail.com
