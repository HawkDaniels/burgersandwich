Many questions have come up on the Southern Pacific email-list that ask if the SP had any GPxx or SDxx units and what paint scheme is appropriate for them. This listing is to give an easy reference for those questions. Most of the time these questions arise from new models that are produced. Some may or may not be available in Southern Pacific paint schemes, and the availability of a unit in SP colors is no guarantee that the colors are right for the modeled unit or that SP ever had one.

This is a listing of all the locomotives that have been made in N scale, and notes on what variations the Southern Pacific had. If the list shows 'NONE' the SP never owned any of this model. If there are specific paint schemes that were used on the units (and not others) these are so noted.

For more information on paint schemes, rosters, photographs and other SP information, see the SP Mailing List FAQ which is hosted by Richard Percy

See Richard's Roster Index for an indication of what SP actually did have on their rosters. There may not be any equivalent model for many of these units. Anyone who has information on kitbashing other units from what has been produced in N scale is welcome to share this information. I will add it to the bottom of the page.

Richard also has a 'work in progress' for each of the paint schemes used by the SP System.

Leonard Ruback is preparing web pages to sort out the SP light clusters and which ones were used on the different units. When these are available, I will add a link to his pages and notes to the listing here.

Note: Modelers designations for the units are used. The information includes data from Jim Semikoski, Richard Percy, Jim Bence and Tom Cataldo. Detail parts for SP are available from Sunrise and others. Additions and corrections are always welcome.

Notes from Richard Percy:

SP already had 340 GP9's by the time this model came out. SP buying the last GP9's ever made, and the only low-nose to a Major Railroad. 20 Phase III's were delivered in Grey and Scarlet as 5872-5891, and built in August and September of 1959.

Bill Shippen notes the following though:
SP *was not* the only major railroad to order and roster factory low nose GP-9's. New York Central was the other (and only) major road to order this type of GP-9.

A high-nose GP18 can be made into a GP9, at least some of the SP/SSW ones came with two 48" fans, as well as four 36" fans. Or a low-nose version can be made into one of the last 20 Phase III GP9's or a GP20 with a bit more work.

SP has never had 'production' SD40-2's or SD45-2's. SP bought the SD40T-2 Tunnel Motor variant instead. EMD doesn't recognize this designation, they call all the variants SD40-2's, which tends to cause confusion.

SP does Roster U28B's but these were 4 Demonstrators that came in U25B carbodies.

There was also a Bloody Nose variation of Gothic SP like the freightcar lettering on some early BN's. The units are not fully documented. Any help or info is welcome.

Some units have Red SP lettering. This will be sorted out soon and added.

Halloween paint of E's and F's and PA's not fully documented, and came in at least two variations, about 12 units total.

Notes from Jim Bence:

The red lettering is actually the white decal that has been burned of by the dynamic brakes which exposes the backing for the Scotch-lite decal.

Some SSW units had yellow hand rails.

Don Jewell notes: The first SP unit painted into the adopted scarlet/grey paint scheme was F7A 6378 in July or August 1958, painted at LA General Shops.
