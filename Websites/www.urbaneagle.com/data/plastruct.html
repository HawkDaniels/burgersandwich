Plastruct Conversions



Whenever I get to the hobby shop to buy supplies, I always seem to forget what size material I need to match what I am modeling. This happens mostly with these small plastic channels, I-beams, H-columns, and such. I created these charts for all of the Plastruct shapes so I could correlate the item number and size with its scale equivalent.

You can use these charts to find the closest size for prototype structural members, or you can convert from magazine articles that give the size in inches for a certain scale. If the article mentions using 5/16 x 3/8 square tubing for a detail in HO scale, you can look up the scale dimensions for that tubing and find the matching tubing for your scale.
