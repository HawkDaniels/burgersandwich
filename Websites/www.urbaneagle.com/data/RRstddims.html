Standard Dimensions - 00-02-22


The charts below are dimensional data that I find useful to have around. There are columns for the converted scale measurements for most scales from Z to F. I also added the A (for Almost) scale of 1:29.

Where a wire gauge or size is given, it is the closest match for the decimal dimension given.

Additional information available on the net: Nelson Kennedy's HO scale specific info

See the main data page for other sites with technical information, mostly related to full size railroad equipment and practices. It is down at the bottom of the web page.
