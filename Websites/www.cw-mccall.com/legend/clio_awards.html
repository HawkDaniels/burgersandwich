Clio Awards

In just about every biography of C.W. McCall, a mention is made of the 1970s television commercials in which the character first appeared, and the fact that several of the commercials won Clio Awards. The Clio Awards are the advertising industry’s equivalent of an Emmy® (for television productions) or an Academy Award® (for motion pictures).
