Convoy 2000

In 1975, C.W. McCall released the biggest hit of his recording career: “Convoy”, from the album Black Bear Road.

In June of 2000, a loosely-organized band of Crispy Critters made a trip from Shaky Town to the Jersey shore. This is their story.
