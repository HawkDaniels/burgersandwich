Rubber Duck Hood Ornament

If you’ve seen the motion picture CONVOY, you’ve noticed the “angry” rubber duck hood ornament on the Mack truck of Martin Penwalt, a.k.a. “Rubber Duck”. Or maybe you’ve seen Death Proof, which also featured the Duck on the hood of a 1970 Chevy Nova.

Now you can have an exact duplicate of that hood ornament, made from the original molds that were used to produce the ducks for the movie. John Billings of Billings Artworks (“The Best Damn Awards Money Can’t Buy”) has these radiator-toppers for sale at Convoy Duck.

Tell John that you read about it on “that C.W. McCall web site”. It won’t get you a discount, but it’ll make us feel good.
