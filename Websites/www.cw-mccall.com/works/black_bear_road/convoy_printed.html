The Works of C.W. McCall : Black Bear Road

Convoy
(C.W. McCall, B. Fries, C. Davis)

This is C.W. McCall’s Greatest Hit. In January 1976, it reached the number one position on both the Billboard Country and Pop charts.

“Convoy” has been covered by many artists, and parodied by many others. It inspired CONVOY, the 1978 motion picture directed by Sam Peckinpah and starring Kris Kristofferson and Ally McGraw. David Frederick has a website devoted to the movie.

These are the lyrics as they’re printed on the album cover, with one difference: the printed lyrics are displayed in ALL UPPERCASE letters. Read the lyrics as transcribed from the album.

An alternate version of the song was written for the motion picture Convoy.

The sequel to “Convoy” is “’Round The World With The Rubber Duck”, which appears on the album Rubber Duck.

“Convoy” can be found on the albums C.W. McCall’s Greatest Hits, The Best of C.W. McCall, and American Spirit.
