City Of New Orleans
(Steve Goodman)

This song was written by folksinger/songwriter Steve Goodman. Its most recognized cover is the version which was recorded by Arlo Guthrie for his 1972 album Hobo’s Lullaby. Coincidentally, the title track “Hobo’s Lullaby” is also covered by C.W. on C.W. McCall & Co.
