Me ’n the boys here we been all the way across the country a number of times, from one coast ta t’other. And we seen a lot of crazy things out there on the road, so we decided to write a song about a group of men that work out there alongside us on the road and never get too much rec-COG-nition. Are ya ready boys?

[Trucker 1]
I will, boy, I will!

Well I ride down the road with a heavy load

(Cheers)

But we don’t want you to think it’s all gophers in the cheek out there on the road, folks. Oh, no sir!

Bring it down a little, fellas, bring it down, I got a scary story to tell. It gets kinda scary out there on the road. Like there was this one time me an’ Milton here, we was at the top of a mountain pass. We are cruisin’ 90 miles an hour down the side of the Rocky Mountains. We got chickens! Chickens stacked 20 feet high, when Milton here realizes that the brakes have given away!

[Milton]
Well, I reckon the brakes have given away, there.

[Trucker 1]
And we were scared, boy, were we scared, Milton?

[Milton]
Well I reckon that the only place we’ll be able to stop is when we hit the wall of Mabel’s Coffee Hut at the bottom of the pass here.

And we’re continuin’ to pick up speed! We’re goin’ 15 hundred-thousand miles an hour down the side of the Rocky Mountains! We got chickens, chickens stacked 2 miles high!
