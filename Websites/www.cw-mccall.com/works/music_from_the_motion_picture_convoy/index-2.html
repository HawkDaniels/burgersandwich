Notes

As the only important song on this album is "Convoy (new version)", I’ve skipped the excessive copyright babble for the other cuts. :)

Trivia: the LP cover picture and the cassette cover picture are identical. In fact, the picture on the cassette cover is a picture of the LP cover, complete with catalog number (it’s very tiny, though). And the copyright information, which occupies three lines of tiny type on the back of the LP, uses up almost half of the cassette’s cover.

David Frederick has a "Convoy" web site that’s devoted to the movie. He’s got clips from the movie and "Convoy (New Version)".
