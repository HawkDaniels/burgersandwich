Comin’ Back For More
(Bill Fries, Chip Davis)

The last original C.W. song. So far. A fast and loose interpretation of an actual event.

Alferd Packer was the only person in the U.S.A. who was ever convicted of cannibalism. One day in the dead of winter he left camp with five men, despite warnings of bad weather. In the spring Al returned to civilization, but without his companions — and with a lot of money.
