Notes

There is no discernable difference between the 1990 and 1999 CD releases. The packaging appears to be identical.

The original recordings of the songs on this album:

  • From Wolf Creek Pass:
    • Glenwood Canyon
    • Night Rider
    • Wolf Creek Pass
    • Rocky Mountain September
  • From Black Bear Road:
    • Ghost Town
    • The Silverton
    • Convoy
    • Black Bear Road
  • From Wilderness:
    • There Won’t Be No Country Music
    • Roy
    • The Little Brown Sparrow
    • Wilderness
    • Aurora Borealis
    • Columbine
  • From Rubber Duck:
    • Camp Bird Mine

The titles of "The Little Brown Sparrow and Me" and "There Won’t Be No Country Music (There Won’t Be No Rock ’N’ Roll)" are shortened on this album, to simply "The Little Brown Sparrow" and "There Won’t Be No Country Music". My speculation: this name change was due to the shortened attention spans of the listeners. :)
