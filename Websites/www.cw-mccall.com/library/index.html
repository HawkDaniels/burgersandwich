Library

Welcome to the Library. Here you'll find links to information about subjects that have been mentioned in the songs of C.W. McCall, arranged in some sort of order. We’re not sure what that order is, though. It's definitely not Dewey Decimal.

Caution: except for pages on this web site, none of these links can be guaranteed to be available. If you do encounter a dead or incorrect link, please report it to the head librarian. All links were checked on 2010 January 29.

  • 8 By 10 Glossy Photo. A publicity still of C.W.
  • Black Bear Road: The Sequel? A photograph that looks a lot like the cover of Black Bear Road.
  • Bob Norton’s Birthday Greetings to Bill Fries. A beautifully designed card that Bob created, and sent to Bill on the occasion of his 70th birthday (15 November 1998).
  • Clio Awards Won By Bill Fries. C.W.'s early days.
  • Concordance A list of place names, people, and a few other items that appear in the songs of C.W. McCall. (In progress.)
  • Convoy: a novel by B.W.L. Norton A tie-in novel for the 1978 motion picture, Convoy.
  • The Dark Of The Moon. When did The Convoy start?
  • Sheet Music. From the first two albums, Wolf Creek Pass and Black Bear Road.
  • Simpsons 10-Code. From The Simpsons episode “Maximum Homerdrive”.
