Monday, 1998 July 6 : Volume 1, Number 6

V-A-C-A-T-I-O-N! Which means that I’m not going to work this week. But I’m not travelling anywhere, either: it’s a stay-at-home-and-do-cheap-stuff vacation. My vow: a new lyric page every day. Today: “Crispy Critters”, from the Wilderness album. Makes me wish that I had a swimmin’ pool.

And thanks to David E. for his corrections to “’Round The World With The Rubber Duck”. Now I know how to say “10-4” in German.

A note to Curious in Chicago: yes, all of the lyrics, so far, have been from the songs on the Greatest Hits album. Only three more to go.
