TECHNIQUE WALL

A. Stance

Low, balanced, relaxed. Tense equals gnarly wipeout.

B. The Slide

Commit, keep your weight centered, let the gear do the work.

C. Stopping

Slow down gradually. Plan your exit before you start.

D. Bailing Out

Falling is part of it. Learn it early, on purpose, at low speed.

GEAR

Helmet, wrist guards, knee and elbow pads, slide-rated gloves or pucks. Totally bodacious, totally mandatory.

☠ SAFETY ☠

🐕 THE DOG SAYS: "Helmet. NOW. Then we can talk, dude."

  • Wear a helmet, wrist guards, and knee and elbow protection every time. No exceptions, no "just one quick slide."
  • Use a smooth, clean, dry surface. Wet, gritty, or cracked concrete is grody.
  • Check the area first: no traffic, no pedestrians, no stairs, no drop-offs.
  • Start slow and low. Practice falling at low speed before you go bigger.
  • Learn from someone experienced, and check your gear for wear before each session.
  • Know local rules about where you can slide.
