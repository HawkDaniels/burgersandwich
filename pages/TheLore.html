Age I: The Garden

Adam and Eve had a son the records omit. John Anime was born already mid-opening-sequence, cursed and blessed with the gift of time. He is the only witness to everything above.

Age II: The Visitors

The Anunnaki came from Nibiru and seeded humanity. Then they heard a sound they could not explain: harsh noise braided with dolphin mating calls. It had no source. It arrived from the future, carried by John Anime.

Age III: The Lost Ages

Lemuria fell, then Atlantis, then the Mud Flood buried Tartaria. The chakras were recolored and Christ consciousness was given a number. The Anunnaki retreated into Agartha with the tape. Evil Wizard was born of a bad summoning and an open White Monster. John Sandwich fed everyone through all of it.

Age IV: The Gambia

The river is the Styx. The dead, the lost, and the very online cross it to reach the dolphin-tuned mouth. John Wizard keeps the banks. Alden Pizza runs the ferry-van and takes slices as toll. Senegal wraps around nearly all of it and belongs to no one. No one crosses it clean.

Age V: The Founding

On February 25th, 1724, twins Pope Barak Music and God Emperor Christopher Music founded Munancho Incorporated in Mecca, Japan. They were born the same day in a Sprite factory. Barak's first words were "I'm cheesing it," and he became The Cheese. Christopher was born as legs with no unbroken bones, and he became The Crunch. Their sound has never been recreated.

Age VI: The Fall

On March 3rd, 2004, Munancho was executed for smuggling fentanyl and Guitar Hero controllers over the US-Azerbaijan border. Hiroshi Abe attended. He nodded once. The last master tape vanished that night. The albums are the legacy. Rest in Piece.

Age VII: The Rift

The Cheese, Chris The Nomad, and Christopher The Crunch were once buddies. The Cheese and the Nomad fell out. The Crunch stepped back and became a monk. Climb on.
