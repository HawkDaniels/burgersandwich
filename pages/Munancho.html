Christopher “The Crunch” Music was famously born as just a pair of legs with zero bones left broken. Munancho was unfortunately executed on March 3rd, 2004 for smuggling fentanyl and Guitar Hero controllers over the US-Azerbaijan border. The albums left… are their legacy. Rest In Piece Munancho, we're all praying for you.
